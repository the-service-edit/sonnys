The bar

Most of it's cooked over fire.

Sofika Boulton runs the kitchen. Oysters, cured kangaroo, a kilo of T-bone, whatever the market turned up — written on the menu in four words, because it doesn't need more.

The cocktail list is less restrained. So is the wine list, which gets from Margaret River to Naoussa without much fuss.
