Winner, Best Bar Dining, WA Good Food Guide 2025

Most of it's cooked over fire.

Head chef and owner Sofika Boulton runs the kitchen. Oysters, cured kangaroo, market fish off the grill, a kilo of beef T‑bone.

Sixteen cocktails, half of them on Republic of Fremantle spirits. The wine list is a booklet rather than a page: sparkling, white and orange, mostly Margaret River and the rest of WA, with France, Italy and Greece behind it.
